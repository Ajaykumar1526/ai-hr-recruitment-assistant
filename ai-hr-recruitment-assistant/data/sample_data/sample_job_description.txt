Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: Nexora Insights
Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-time

About the Role:
We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our analytics team, responsible for
building dashboards, analyzing business data, and supporting data-driven
decision making across the company.

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau for business stakeholders
- Write SQL queries to extract and analyze data from our warehouse
- Use Python/Pandas for data cleaning and exploratory analysis
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders
- Collaborate with engineering to improve data pipeline reliability

Required Skills:
- SQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Data visualization (Power BI or Tableau)
- Statistics fundamentals

Preferred Skills:
- Excel (advanced)
- Airflow or another orchestration tool
- Experience with A/B testing

Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or related field
- 1-3 years of experience in a data analyst or similar role

Minimum Experience: 1 year
